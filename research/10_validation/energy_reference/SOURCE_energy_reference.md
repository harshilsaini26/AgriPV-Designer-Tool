| Units and key column meanings | `gsa_pvout_kwh_per_kwp_yr`: GSA's AC output per kWp of DC capacity for an open-field plant on a fixed rack at the optimum tilt (`gsa_opt_tilt_deg`), with Solargis's default losses. `gsa_ghi_kwh_m2_yr`: GSA global horizontal irradiation. `measured_kwh_per_kw_yr` with `measured_basis`: `kWp_dc` (per kW of modules) or `kw_ac` (per kW of inverter or grid capacity). `measured_vs_gsa`: measured ÷ GSA; for AC-rated plants it is a range for an assumed DC/AC ratio of 1.25–1.40 |
| Known caveats and gaps | (1) **Results.** GSA PVOUT runs from 1,410 kWh/kWp/yr (Kamrup) and 1,451 (Lucknow) to 1,752 (Jodhpur) and 1,812 (Leh). Measured against GSA: **Pavagada 0.95–1.07** (six years averaging 2,174 kWh/kW AC, CUF 24.8%), **GIPCL 0.84–0.95**, **CIAL 0.96** (weak), and **CAZRI 0.72**. The large plants sit within the D18 ±7% band only if their DC/AC ratio is known; neither KSPDCL nor GIPCL publishes it. (2) **CAZRI is not a standard plant.** It is a 100 kWp research system with rows at different densities and crops between them, and 2021 output may include outages and soiling; it is a floor for agrivoltaic layouts, not a check on the open-field model. (3) **Pavagada output fell** from 4,599 MU (2023-24) to 4,219 MU (2025-26), about 8%, with no reason given (curtailment, soiling or outages are possible); the six-year mean is used. (4) **GIPCL's site list is not on the page.** The 262 MW fleet is assumed to be its north Gujarat parks (Charanka, Raghanesda); FY 2025-26 onwards adds new capacity part-year and is excluded. (5) **CIAL figures are press round numbers** (1.6 lakh kWh/day from 40 MWp; 2 lakh from 50 MWp, which includes a plant in Kannur district). (6) **Not found:** measured output for Rewa (MP) and Bhadla (Rajasthan), and any measured KUSUM-A plant data. Regulator orders assume normative CUFs on an AC basis: MPERC 21%, TGERC 19%, OERC 18.7%, JSERC 20% (`../../11_finance_policy/kusum/kusum_a_state_tariffs.csv`); a 1.25 DC/AC plant at Jodhpur's GSA output would reach about 25% |
| Processing notes | `research/_scripts/energy_reference.py`. Districts were chosen to span climates: hot desert (Jodhpur), dry coast (Kachchh), semi-arid south (Anantapur, Tumakuru), foggy and hazy plains (Ludhiana, Lucknow), humid tropics (Ernakulam), wet north-east (Kamrup), central plateau (Rewa) and high mountains (Leh). Because the calibrated typical year (D14) matches GSA annual irradiation at these points, a gap between the app's energy model and GSA PVOUT points at the PV model (losses, temperature, tilt), not the weather. Monthly values are GSA daily PVOUT times days in the month and sum to the annual value |
