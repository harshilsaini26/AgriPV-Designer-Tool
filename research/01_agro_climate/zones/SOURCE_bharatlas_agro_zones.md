| Known caveats and gaps | (1) Macro zones only: the 73 ACZ sub-zones and the 60 AESRs have no open polygons; ICAR-CRIDA district contingency plans list them per district (see `SOURCE_CANDIDATES.md`). (2) **Coarse boundaries**: the crosswalk finds only 17 districts with two or more ACZs ≥ 5% and 128 with two or more AERs. Districts that official texts describe as straddling zones (e.g. Mysuru across ACZ X and XII in its CRIDA plan) come out ≥ 99% in one zone here, so treat the polygon assignment as a default and let CRIDA Section 1.1 override it where they differ. (3) 6 coastal / island districts have < 95% coverage because the coarse coastline misses land (Lakshadweep 81%, Nicobars 85%, Diu 88%, Anand 91%). (4) The text fields contain encoding errors ("�C") and HTML entities ("&amp;"). (5) The AER numbering is the 1992 scheme, not the 2015 revision (NBSS&LUP Publ. 170: 19 AERs, islands merged); label the version in the UI |
