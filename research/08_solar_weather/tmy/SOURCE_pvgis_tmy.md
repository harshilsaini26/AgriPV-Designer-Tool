| Known caveats and gaps | (1) **Total sunlight is close nationally but biased regionally.** The median district is −0.9% against GSA, but **237 of 785 districts are outside ±5%**. The typical year is too sunny across the Indo-Gangetic plain (Delhi +11%, Punjab and Haryana +7%, Uttar Pradesh +6%; 43 of 75 UP districts flagged) and in Ladakh (+23%). It is too dull in the wettest and hilliest states (Kerala −7%, Sikkim −7%, Meghalaya −6%). This fits ERA5's known weak representation of winter haze and fog over the plains and of monsoon cloud. (2) **Diffuse light is far too low.** Median diffuse share is **0.31** in the typical year against **0.49** in the Global Solar Atlas and **0.45** in NASA POWER (`../solar_atlas/`). Annual diffuse is 37% below GSA in the median district. ERA5 sends too much light as direct beam, which would exaggerate shadows under panels and understate diffuse light on crops and module backs. **Decision needed** on whether to calibrate before the physics pipeline uses these files (see below). (3) **Leh's point is not in Leh's farmland.** The official Leh district polygon includes Gilgit-Baltistan, and the WorldCover cropland centroid falls there (35.61° N, 74.62° E, 1,296 m). Leh's typical year (+26% against GSA) therefore describes the wrong place; pick a point in the Indus valley near Leh before use. Kargil also reads high against GSA. (4) A typical year has no year-to-year variability; P90-style risk needs the full 2005–2023 series |
| Processing notes | `research/_scripts/pvgis_tmy_districts.py` (stdlib fetch with retries; GSA sampled with `sample_gsa_districts.sample`). Checks: Jodhpur gives 2,024 kWh/m²/yr against GSA 2,032 (−0.4%), with a diffuse share of 0.27 annually (0.22 in December, 0.44 in July). **Calibration options for the pipeline:** (a) use as is; (b) scale hourly GHI to GSA annual GHI (`ghi_scale_to_gsa`), rescale DHI to GSA annual diffuse, and recompute DNI from closure (GHI − DHI) / cos(zenith); (c) switch to NASA POWER hourly (CERES/MERRA-2), whose diffuse share agrees better with GSA but whose grid is coarser (about 0.5–1°) |
