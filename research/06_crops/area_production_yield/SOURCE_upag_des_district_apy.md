| Units and key column meanings | Long files: `State`, `District`, `Crop Year`, `Estimation Cycle`, `Season` (Kharif/Rabi/Summer/Total), `Crop Code`, `Crop` (39 crops and aggregates, e.g. Rice, Wheat, Soybean, Rapeseed & Mustard, Cotton, Sugarcane, Total Food Grains), `Crop Category`, `Crop Sub-Category` (`\|`-joined if several), `Metric` (Area / Production / Yield), `Value`, `Unit Of Measure` (Ha, Tonnes, Kg/Ha; **cotton production in bales of 170 kg; jute, mesta and sannhemp in bales of 180 kg**), `Total Type` (Crop-wise Data / District Total / State Total) |
| Known caveats and gaps | (1) **No horticulture:** onion, potato, tomato, other vegetables, fruit and spices are missing, yet they are prime agrivoltaic candidates, so they need NHB / DA&FW horticulture statistics (district level where available). (2) Aggregate rows (Cereals, Total Pulses, Total Food Grains, District Total, State Total) sit alongside crop rows; filter `Total Type == "Crop-wise Data"` and exclude aggregate crop names before summing. (3) District names follow DES spelling, so map them to LGD via the D00 alias table. (4) District sets change across years as districts split. (5) Zero area with a non-zero yield appears for tiny areas because of rounding in the source. (6) **Season labels and totals change in 2023-24.** In 2019-20 to 2022-23 the eastern states' main-season (winter / Aman) rice is labelled **Rabi**: about 115 lakh ha in West Bengal, Odisha, Bihar and Assam, which puts national rabi rice at about 160 lakh ha against a real 40–45. Relabel rice "Rabi" as Kharif for any state that reports no rabi rice in 2023-24. In the same years district wheat sums to about 350 lakh ha against an All-India final of about 310, mostly from Madhya Pradesh (98 vs 75 lakh ha in 2023-24), Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan. 2023-24 and 2024-25 reconcile with the All-India final estimates (rice 478.2 and wheat 318.2 lakh ha in 2023-24), so build baselines from those years and treat earlier years as shares only |
