| Units and key column meanings | CAPEX in **₹ per kWp DC**: MPERC's ₹347.04 lakh per MW AC divided by its 1.10 DC/AC ratio gives ₹31,550/kWp DC, then times each preset's multiplier. O&M in ₹ per kWp DC in year 1, escalating 5.25% a year. `grade` rates the evidence: B (regulator), C (Indian vendor or study plus international), D (one foreign estimate) |
| Known caveats and gaps | (1) **Prices are from 2024.** Module prices have fallen since (Rajasthan cut its KUSUM-A tariff in Nov 2025 for that reason), so the base may now be high. Refresh from the next SERC order. (2) The **agrivoltaic premiums rest mainly on one Indian vendor's figures** (SunSeed APV, an undated web article), cross-checked against CSTEP's +23%. The German (Fraunhofer, +51% to +76%) and US (NREL, +50% fixed tilt) figures set the high end. (3) **Tracker and vertical bifacial evidence is thin.** JMK Research and SgurrEnergy (2026) disagree on whether trackers cost more per unit of output. Vertical bifacial rests on one NREL estimate. (4) **O&M multipliers for elevated (×1.10) and tracker (×1.20) structures are assumptions**: Fraunhofer notes that cleaning and repairs at height cost more, but gives no number. (5) The base includes a 5 km 11 kV line and bay (₹23 lakh/MW); plants closer to a substation cost less. (6) Monofacial and bifacial modules use the same CAPEX in the default, as Indian price differences are small; Fraunhofer's +€326/kWp is for special German modules |
| Processing notes | Height premiums for fixed tilt are a straight line through SunSeed's 1.25 m (+7.8%) and 3.75 m (+30.8%) figures: +19% at 2.5 m, +24% at 3 m, +33% at 4 m. The tracker premiums interpolate SunSeed's 1.75 m (+23.5%) and 3.75 m (+37.8%) tracker figures. Other finance parameters in the same MPERC order: CUF 21%, auxiliary use 0.75%, debt:equity 70:30, loan 15 years at 10.63%, RoE 14% (pre-tax 16.81% to year 20, 19.40% after), salvage 10%, depreciation 4.67%/yr for 15 years then 2%/yr. Lease rent is in `../land_lease/lease_benchmarks.csv` |
