| Known caveats and gaps | (1) **State and district names lost their spaces in the PDF text** ("UTTARPRADESH", "AlluriSitharamaRaju"); match to LGD with a whitespace-insensitive comparison (no LGD codes in the report). (2) District stage of extraction hides hotspots: use the unit-category table (116 districts have ≥ 50% of their units critical or over-exploited in 2025). (3) Unit definitions changed between editions in some states (e.g. Tamil Nadu firkas → taluks), so compare district totals across years, not units. (4) 13 rows (2025) / 25 rows (2024), mostly Punjab, have recharge − discharge ≠ extractable resource; this is a feature of the source (environmental-flow and other adjustments), not a parsing error |
| Processing notes | `research/_scripts/parse_cgwb_annexure2.py` (district resources) and `research/_scripts/parse_cgwb_annexure3b.py` (unit categories), PyMuPDF. The PDF text layer moves each decimal point to the end of the number ("3049859\n." for 30498.59; "100\n." for 1.00), so cells are rebuilt from characters ordered by x position. **Validation (2025):** district sums give **407.75 bcm** extractable resource and **60.63%** stage of extraction, exactly the report's national figures; unit counts sum to **6,762** with Safe 4,946 / Semi-critical 758 / Critical 201 / Over-exploited 730 / Saline 127, exactly the executive-summary table; extraction components sum to the total and stage = extraction ÷ resource in every row. 2024 sums give 60.48% (published 60.47%). 94 districts are over-exploited (> 100%) in 2025, vs 102 in 2024 |
